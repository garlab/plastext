## $f: \Bbb N \to \Bbb N$

The same formula at body size: $f: \Bbb N \to \Bbb N$.

### Recurrence with blank lines

$$
\begin{aligned}
f(0) &= 0 \\

f(n+1) &= f(n) + 1 \\

\text{so } f(n) &= n
\end{aligned}
$$
